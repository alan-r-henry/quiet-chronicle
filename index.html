Quiet Chronicle uploader

A private tool for one YouTube channel: The Quiet Chronicle.

The Quiet Chronicle is a YouTube channel of slow, gentle history narration to fall asleep to. Each episode is about an hour long.

The Quiet Chronicle uploader is a small program that runs on the channel owner's own computer. When an episode is finished and has been reviewed by a person, it uploads the episode to the channel through YouTube API Services (the YouTube Data API), with its title, description, thumbnail, English captions and playlist.

Who can use it

Only the owner of The Quiet Chronicle, for that channel only. It is not offered to anyone else, it has no other users, and it is not sold or distributed.

What it does on YouTube

  • Uploads the channel's own finished videos (videos.insert).
  • Sets each video's thumbnail (thumbnails.set) and adds its English captions (captions.insert).
  • Adds the video to one of the channel's playlists (playlists.list, playlistItems.insert).

It does not read, collect or store data about viewers or other channels, and it shows no YouTube content.
